# Target Journal Rules: IEEE TVLSI

Audited 1 September 2026. The journal FAQ currently linked by TVLSI is dated
2019; requirements must be rechecked in ScholarOne immediately before
submission because venue rules and charges can change.

## TVLSI-specific preparation

- Primary target: *IEEE Transactions on Very Large Scale Integration (VLSI)
  Systems*.
- Regular papers may be at most 14 pages including biographies; briefs may be
  at most 5 pages.
- Overlength charges apply above 9 pages for regular papers and above 4 pages
  for briefs.
- TVLSI is not a blind-submission venue; author names belong in the manuscript.
- Biographies are required for regular papers.
- An extension of earlier publication must contain at least 30% new material
  and disclose the relationship/differences.
- Use IEEE two-column format with figures embedded in the manuscript.
- The reviewed and accepted versions should be materially consistent in length
  and content.

Primary journal source: <https://tvlsi.ieee.org/otherdocs/2019_05_faq.pdf>.

## IEEE-wide preparation

- Use the IEEE journal template/IEEEtran class.
- Keep the abstract self-contained, in one paragraph, at no more than 250
  words. Do not put citations, equations, footnotes, or undefined
  abbreviations in it.
- Supply three to five index terms.
- The corresponding author requires an ORCID; IEEE requests ORCIDs for all
  authors.
- Cite all reused or adapted information and graphics. Secure permission where
  required and include a credit line.
- Disclose AI-generated article content in the acknowledgments, naming the
  system and the affected sections/level of use. The authors remain responsible
  for accuracy, originality, citations, and final approval.
- Prepare supplementary material so it is understandable and citable from the
  article.
- Follow IEEE reproducibility guidance by making code, scripts, seeds, and
  report provenance accessible.

## Draft decisions

- Manuscript type: regular-paper working draft; not yet ready for submission.
- Page target: 9 pages including references/biographies if the evidence can be
  strengthened, to avoid relying on the 14-page hard ceiling.
- Figures: original vector artwork, readable in grayscale, with editable
  `.drawio` sources and no screenshots from the private specification.
- Results: tool/version/device/configuration stated adjacent to quantitative
  values; negative results retained.
- Author metadata: placeholder only until supplied and approved by the authors.
